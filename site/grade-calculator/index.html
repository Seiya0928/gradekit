How it works

How to calculate it

Add all points you earned, add all points possible, then divide the two totals. This points-based method works when assignments are not separated into weighted categories.

Current grade = (total points earned ÷ total points possible) × 100

Worked example

Example calculation

If you earned 87 points out of 100 possible points, your current grade is 87%, typically a B+ on a standard U.S. scale.

Common questions

Grade Calculator FAQ

What grade scale does this calculator use?

The letter-grade label uses a common U.S. plus/minus scale. Your school may use different cutoffs, so the percentage is the authoritative result.

Can I include extra credit?

Yes. Add extra-credit points to points earned. If the assignment adds points without increasing points possible, enter 0 as its possible points.

Is my grade data stored?

No. Every calculation happens in your browser and GradeKit does not send or save your entries.
