How it works

How to calculate it

Subtract wrong answers from total questions. Divide correct answers by total questions and multiply by 100. The chart repeats this for every possible number wrong.

Score = [(total questions − wrong answers) ÷ total questions] × 100

Worked example

Example calculation

On a 50-question test with 4 wrong answers, the student has 46 correct. The score is 46 ÷ 50 × 100 = 92%, typically an A−.

Use the calculator above to substitute your own numbers. Results update immediately.

Common questions

EZ Grader FAQ

Can I print the EZ Grader chart?

Yes. Use your browser’s Print command. GradeKit includes a clean print layout and does not require a separate PDF download.

Can I change the letter-grade scale?

The MVP uses a common U.S. plus/minus scale. Use the percentage column if your school has different cutoffs.

What is the maximum test length?

The interface supports up to 500 questions to keep the generated chart practical.
